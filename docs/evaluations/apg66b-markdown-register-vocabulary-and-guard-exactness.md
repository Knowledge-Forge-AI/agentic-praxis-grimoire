## G4 — numeric-policy polarity

The guard rejects normative numeric thresholds or bands, count-selected
warning levels, count-triggered signals or responses, and count-authorized
splits, including hyphenated, spaced, ranged, backticked, parenthetical, and
table-cell forms. Descriptive counts, negative examples, statements that no
threshold exists, and statements that count does not classify, fire a signal,
or select a response remain allowed. This is targeted lexical enforcement, not
general natural-language parsing.

## G5 — exact closed tokens

One lexical primitive accepts a token only when neither adjacent character is
an ASCII letter, digit, underscore, or hyphen. It is shared by owners,
selection/response axes, routes, structural and semantic signals, rollback
literals, and source-boundary literals. Exact plain, backticked, punctuation-
delimited, and table-cell tokens pass; prefixed, suffixed, hyphenated, and
underscored larger strings do not contribute the closed token.
