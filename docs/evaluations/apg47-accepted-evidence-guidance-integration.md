## Failing-first and correction

Exact APG45 does not force an implied completion inference to be stated as its
own claim and does not distinguish unsupported taste from grounded evaluative
judgment. Exact APG46 adds those accepted owner surfaces but overbroadly turns
all judgment into advice and lets counterevidence justify a finding before its
quality is evaluated.

Before normative correction, nineteen focused contracts ran against the
byte-identical APG46 skill: ten passed and nine failed. The named adverse cases
covered unsupported taste, policy-grounded evaluation, demonstrated structural
coupling, an incomplete operational rollback, weak counterevidence, strong
counterevidence, mixed counterevidence, material implied inference, and the
complete counterevidence disposition boundary. The routine no-ceremony control
passed.

One coherent forward correction:

- reserves advice for unsupported preference or taste;
- permits a judgment or interpretation to remain a finding when repository
  policy, an accepted contract, observable structure, demonstrated behavior, or
  a named risk supports it;
- treats a forecast as a forecast without suppressing a current bounded-risk
  judgment;
- narrows, defers, or states the limitation of an assertion with no defined
  check;
- triggers evidence direction from an unestablished material claim;
- evaluates counterevidence for relevance, directness, lineage,
  method/reliability, strength, and scope; and
- permits a finding, unresolved disposition, narrower claim, request for more
  evidence, or no change without automatic refutation or evidentiary priority.

Independent corrected-state review found no new material defect.
