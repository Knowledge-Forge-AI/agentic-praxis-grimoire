## `docs-only-change-hygiene`

APG structured defaults and repository-local rules cover:

- formal docs-only phases with proportional validation and retained phase
  records;
- ordinary non-phase docs-only work without formal ceremony;
- stricter local documentation policy;
- complete-diff, whitespace, local-link, and applicable documentation checks;
- deliberate non-execution and disclosure of unrelated source tests; and
- authority-preserving non-triggers for behavior changes and unapproved
  commits or phases.

No private-only behavior was found in the personal target. Exact restoration
and unrelated-state preservation were proven, and fresh non-author review
accepted the resulting decommission.

Disposition: **Decommissioned.**
