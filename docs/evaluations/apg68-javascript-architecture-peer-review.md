## Decision and narrowings

ADR 0039 is Rejected. The corrected architecture is preserved as historical
rejected evidence and is not current architecture input. Eligibility is
`not-applicable-rejected`. The ten proposed narrowings below remain review
evidence only, not a future contract:

1. question-specific source authority and version-bound implementation facts;
2. no Node, browser/DOM, or toolchain ownership by the JavaScript profile;
3. no TypeScript or JSX whole-file selection;
4. no numeric whole-file bands;
5. no automatic JavaScript-to-TypeScript migration;
6. no target-calibrated structural claim;
7. Annex B only under an established context;
8. no promised host ordering or finalization timing;
9. browser-page specificity for the frozen timer/fetch scenario; and
10. repository-owned produced-artifact permission with exact provenance and
    regeneration ownership.

Under the one-correction rule, APG68 made no second semantic repair. This
decision grants no candidate-authoring authority, and APG69 is not
recommended.
