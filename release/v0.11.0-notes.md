## Qualification and release boundary

Local candidate builds and checks do not publish packages. Public staging
discloses its contents, so confidentiality, rights, exact projection, and
private-history checks precede its push. The future promotion uses `staging`
→ PR → squash merge to `main` → release. Actual merged source and commit-bound
artifacts must be qualified before tagging and publication.

`public_staging: not_started`; `public_pr_ci: not_run`; `public_release: not_started`.

Use a source checkout or locally qualified candidate packages while v0.11.0
is unreleased. v0.10.0 remains the published version. See
[distribution guidance](../docs/distribution.md) and the
[release procedure](../docs/public-release-process.md). Cross-built Linux
artifacts are not evidence of Linux execution or full developer qualification.
